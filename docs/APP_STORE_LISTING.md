## Description (4000 char max)

```
HOU2ED connects people looking for housing with housing providers — shelters, transitional housing programs, and other facilities — in one app.

FOR HOUSING SEEKERS
• Search and filter available housing listings
• Save your searches to quickly check back for new matches
• Apply directly in the app with a secure digital signature
• Message providers and track your application status
• Get a clear timeline of every application you've submitted

FOR HOUSING PROVIDERS
• Post and manage your available listings
• Update bed/unit availability in real time
• Review incoming applications and respond to applicants
• Message applicants directly through the app

SAFETY & TRUST
• Block or report any user directly from a conversation
• Digitally signed applications with timestamp and identity verification, enforceable under the E-SIGN Act
• Your information is only shared with the providers you choose to apply to — never sold, never shared otherwise

HOU2ED is a technology platform that connects housing seekers with housing providers. It is not itself a housing provider, shelter operator, or property manager — see the in-app Terms of Service for details on how the platform works.

Whether you're looking for a place to stay or managing housing availability for others, HOU2ED is built to make the process faster, clearer, and safer for everyone involved.
```
(~1,250 chars — well under the 4,000 limit; room to expand later with real screenshots/testimonials once available.)
