PRIVACY POLICY

This Privacy Policy explains how HOU2ED LLC ("HOU2ED," "we," "our," "us") collects, uses, stores, and protects your information when you use our mobile application, website, or any related services ("Services").

By creating an account or using HOU2ED, you agree to this Privacy Policy.

Information We Collect

1. Information You Provide

We collect information you submit when you:

  • Create an account
  • Build a user or provider profile
  • Apply for housing or services
  • Upload documents
  • Send messages
  • Submit digitally signed forms

This may include:

  • Name, contact information
  • Date of birth
  • Government-issued ID
  • Insurance information
  • Housing history
  • Application responses
  • Uploaded documents (PDFs, photos, forms, etc.)

2. Information Collected Automatically

We may automatically collect:

  • Device information
  • IP address
  • Session activity
  • Crash logs
  • Usage analytics

This helps ensure performance, safety, and security.

3. Information from Providers or Agencies

When you apply to a housing provider, organization, or hospital, they may share:

  • Application status
  • Document requirements
  • Verification results
  • Eligibility decisions
  • Intake notes or denial reasons

We never share your information with any provider unless you send an application to them.

How We Use Information

We use your information to:

  • Operate and improve the HOU2ED platform
  • Match users with available housing options
  • Facilitate communication
  • Process applications and documents
  • Generate intake packets
  • Verify identity
  • Maintain platform safety
  • Provide customer support

We do not sell personal information.

How We Share Information

We may share information only when:

  1. You Apply to a Provider: We transmit information only to the organizations you intentionally select.
  2. We Use Secure Service Providers: For storage, authentication, hosting, analytics, or messaging.
  3. Legal Requirements Apply: We may disclose information if compelled by subpoena, court order, or applicable law.

Data Security

We use reasonable safeguards, including:

  • Encryption in transit and at rest
  • Role-based access control
  • Time-stamped document verification
  • Audit logs
  • Secure cloud storage
